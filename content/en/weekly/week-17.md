### AI / Models / Agents

[Soofi S — Sovereign German-English Foundation Model](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Soofi-Project/Pretraining-Tech-Report) — Soofi Project's pretraining report for a German–English foundation model.

[Learning from experience instead of curated datasets](https://oaklab.ai/posts/learning-from-experience-instead-of-curated-datasets) · [Oak Lab](https://oaklab.ai/) — a discussion of how agents can learn from experience rather than curated datasets.

[Inkling: Our Open-Weights Model](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling/) — an open-weights model released by Thinking Machines Lab.

[Have Chinese AI Models Caught Up to the US Frontier?](https://scaling01.substack.com/p/have-chinese-ai-models-caught-up) — uses benchmarks and capability-gap estimates to ask whether Chinese models have caught the US frontier.

[[2607.10252] One Token Is Enough: Fingerprinting and Verifying Large Language Models from Single-Token Output Distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10252) — fingerprints and verifies LLMs from single-token output distributions, useful when the serving chain is opaque.

[Introducing Laguna S 2.1](https://poolside.ai/blog/introducing-laguna-s-2-1) — Poolside's Laguna S 2.1 release, emphasizing longer-horizon work and more effective use of reasoning.

[Macaron-V1-Preview: 749B MoL Agent Model post-trained from GLM5.1](https://macaron.im/mindlab/research/macaron-v1-preview) — a 749B MoL agent model preview post-trained from GLM5.1.

[[2507.09369] A Taxonomy of Omnicidal Futures Involving Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09369) — a taxonomy and examples of AI-related futures in which nearly all humans are killed; framed as possibilities, not inevitabilities.

[Scaling Video Pretraining with Imagination Models](https://www.inductionlabs.com/news/scaling-video-pretraining) — Induction Labs pretrained Photon-1 from scratch on 575 million frames of computer-use data, using autoregressive training in latent space.

[[2607.20548] SOAP, Muon, and Beyond: Pushing LLM Pretraining Scales](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20548) · [NVIDIA-NeMo/Emerging-Optimizers](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Emerging-Optimizers) — adapts and strengthens higher-order optimizers such as SOAP and Muon for large-scale pretraining, with related NVIDIA NeMo implementations.

[How to train a frontier-level world model](https://next-state.github.io/open-dreamer/) · [next-state/open-dreamer](https://github.com/next-state/open-dreamer) — an open-source Dreamer implementation in JAX.

[Causal Video Models Are Data-Efficient Robot Policy Learners](https://www.rhoda.ai/research/direct-video-action) — Rhoda AI's DVA reformulates robot policies as video generation for better data efficiency and long-context behavior.

[What is the purpose of interpretability?](https://ericjmichaud.com/interp-purpose/) — asks what interpretability research is for and what problems it should serve.
