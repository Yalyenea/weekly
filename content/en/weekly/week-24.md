### Mathematics / Research / Learning

[Robot Learning | ETH Zürich](https://cvg.ethz.ch/lectures/Robot-Learning/): A course covering imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and policy optimization through to vision-language-action and robotics foundation models, with slides, recordings, and practical assignments.

[Moon | Bartosz Ciechanowski](https://ciechanow.ski/): Interactive explanations of the Moon’s orbit, tidal locking, phases, and solar and lunar eclipses.

[Tristan Buckmaster’s statement](https://cims.nyu.edu/~tristanb/statement.pdf): Buckmaster describes results on fluid-equation blowup under smooth forcing obtained with Levent Alpöge using LLMs, alongside the research process and publication dispute; subsequently, [On the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/) announced a proof and Lean formalization of finite-time blowup for the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations under smooth forcing, stating that it establishes formulations C and D of the Millennium Prize Problem.

![image.png|250](/images/3efe9f7632df0148.png)

[AlphaGenome | Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-ai-for-better-understanding-the-genome/): Predicts molecular properties related to gene regulation from long DNA sequences and assesses variant effects by comparing predictions before and after a mutation.

[The geometry of nonergodic composition — Belief Updates](https://simplex.pub/nonergodic-geometry/): A discussion of the geometry of nonergodic composition—quite elaborate, and I haven’t made sense of it yet.

[Declaration — Math and AI](https://mathandai.org/): A declaration on AI and mathematics that emphasizes conceptual understanding, sharing methods, and training researchers, while questioning progress measured by the number and speed of solved problems.

[Games | OpenAI Developers](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/games): A showcase of games built with OpenAI tools, with projects and development write-ups.
